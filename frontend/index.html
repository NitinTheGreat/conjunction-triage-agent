Two datasets. Two distinct questions.

TraCSS supplies close-approach geometry for the scene and distributions. Kelvins supplies anonymised risk histories for triage and evaluation. They are not linked event by event.

About the data & its limits

TraCSS Conjunction Assessment IV&V data, produced by The Aerospace Corporation for the US Office of Space Commerce (CC0-1.0), combines catalogued objects with synthetic cases.

Each position is evaluated at that encounter’s TCA. This is neither a live feed nor a simultaneous snapshot of all objects.

The 10⁻¹⁰ reporting floor is inferred from the data. Censored entries are treated as bounds, not exact probabilities. An unavailable Pc may reflect a non-positive-definite covariance.

The Spherical answer key uses a 10 km screening sphere and 0.5 m hard-body radii; SFSH uses per-object volumes. The files can contain overlapping encounters.

The dataset was designed to validate screening geometry. Reproducing a probability calculated with the same method is a numerical check, not validation against actual collisions.

Research use only. Public orbital data must not be used here for operational collision avoidance.

How is uncertainty oriented?

UVW rotates with each orbit: radial, along-track and cross-track. ECI uses fixed inertial axes. Positions and velocities above are always J2000/ECI.

Covariance entries are variances (squared uncertainties). Cross-covariances are set to zero in this form.

Why the frame matters

Local covariances must each be rotated into ECI before they can be combined. Selecting the wrong frame can produce a plausible but different number without an error. For these default inputs, the difference is about 6×; other geometries can differ by orders of magnitude.

The defaults use uncertainty magnitudes from an SFSH example, with dominant along-track uncertainty; the state vectors are illustrative.

What has the physics engine established?

The Alfano (2004) implementation was checked on 20,000 sampled TraCSS records. After excluding 8,941 censored values, all 11,059 comparable values agreed with the published probability within 0.1%; the median ratio was approximately 0.999994.

This reproduces the published calculation. It does not validate collision predictions against real outcomes. Each live response includes its method, code version, time and limitations.

Read the risk scale

Risk is log₁₀(Pc). A value of −5 means a probability of 0.00001 (1 in 100,000). More negative values mean smaller probabilities.

Agent scope, evidence & dataset limits

The agent acts as a corrector when the latest visible log-risk is at least −7.0. Below that threshold, the baseline is used without an agent call. Inputs stop at least two days before TCA.

A CDM (Conjunction Data Message) is a report of an encounter’s estimated geometry, risk and uncertainty. A series contains successive reports about one encounter.

Kelvins has anonymised event identifiers and relative times, not absolute timestamps or catalogue identities. These events cannot be matched to objects in the 3D scene. A “high-risk” label describes the final calculated probability, not an observed collision.

Agent confidence is self-reported, not a calibrated probability of correctness. A live request is separate from the stored Phase 7 evaluation.

KEEP THE MEANING IN VIEW

A guide to the workspace

You do not need an orbital mechanics background to follow the demo. Start with these distinctions.

Conjunction
A predicted close approach between two space objects. A close approach does not by itself imply a collision.
TCA / miss distance
Time of closest approach / the separation between the predicted object positions at that time.
Pc
Calculated probability of collision, conditional on geometry, sizes and the uncertainty model. 10⁻⁴ = 0.01% = 1 in 10,000.
Covariance
A matrix describing the size and direction of position uncertainty. It describes uncertainty about an object’s position, not its physical size.
Hard-body radius
A radius representing the physical extent of an object. The two radii are added for the collision calculation.
Dilution
When spreading uncertainty further can make the calculated probability smaller. A small Pc can therefore accompany poor knowledge. An unflagged covariance is not guaranteed accurate.
Censored / unavailable
Censored means a value is at a reporting floor and is treated as a bound. Unavailable means no probability was reported. Neither means zero risk.
CDM / baseline
A Conjunction Data Message is one encounter report. The latest-CDM baseline uses the most recent visible reported risk as its prediction.
Risk thresholds
The scene uses 10⁻⁴ as a probability reference. Kelvins sends latest log-risk ≥ −7 to the agent; its evaluation calls final log-risk ≥ −6 high-risk. These are different definitions, not universal action rules.
Evaluation loss (L)
The Kelvins score combines prediction error on high-risk labels with detection performance. Lower is better. It is not the probability of a collision.
Geometry, distributions and stored results work without a backend. The calculator needs the API; live agent triage additionally needs data and an LLM credential. The recorded example is explicitly labelled.
