## Decisions produced by this reading

1. Use the whole event/sequence as the calibration unit. Do not claim within-sequence correlation alone disproves event-level conformal validity.
2. Keep the default study retrospective plus newly held-out simulation; postpone independent real efficacy claims until access and precision gates pass.
3. Use a transparent supervised partition-summary model. Its range is sensitivity to selected partitions, not a proven confidence interval or identified set.
4. Start lineage validation with a small analytic observation-window harness. Orbital validation remains a separate, larger task.
5. Make the default harm bound exact and conservative: new misses upper-bound net added misses. More efficient paired methods are conditional on validation, not selected after seeing favorable p-values.
6. Separate model fitting, probability calibration, optional risk certification and efficacy evaluation. The same scarce positives cannot count independently at all four stages.

The proposed weighting formulas, simulator construction, contrast, workload bound and power grid are research-design choices in Report 02, not reported achievements of the cited papers. The zero-error bounds were checked arithmetically; no experiment was run to claim the proposed method works.
