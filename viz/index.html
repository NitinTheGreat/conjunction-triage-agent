About this data

Real catalogued objects from the TraCSS Conjunction Assessment IV&V dataset (The Aerospace Corporation for the US Office of Space Commerce, CC0-1.0). Positions are at time of closest approach — this is not a live feed and nothing is propagated.
The 1e-10 Pc floor is inferred from the data, not documented. The Users Guide states only that Pc may be NULL when covariance is non-positive-definite.
This dataset validates screening geometry; it is not a Pc benchmark. The guide states directly that "direct comparison of Pc values requires the same method of Pc computation and is therefore not a key metric of this dataset."
Not for operational use. Public orbital data must never be used for operational collision avoidance.
Every chart is labelled population when it is drawn from all — ingested events, or sample when it is drawn from the —-event stratified sample. The sample deliberately over-represents rare high-probability events so they are visible at all — never read a dataset proportion off a sample chart.
