# Bundled globe coastline

`land-110m.json` is the unmodified land geometry from **world-atlas 2.0.2**, which redistributes Natural Earth 4.1.0 land boundaries at 1:110 million scale.

- Upstream project: https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas
- Download: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/world-atlas@2.0.2/land-110m.json
- Natural Earth: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
- Downloaded: 2026-09-09
- SHA-256: `ead5f68119c49a9250902e7da303bcb209341bbb8fefe7369a439b48b704658a`
- Redistribution license: see `LICENSE.world-atlas` (ISC). Natural Earth cartographic data are in the public domain.

The application fetches this **bundled local file**, never a third-party map service. The coastline is used for an illustrative Earth texture. Procedural contour lines, colors and lighting are decorative; they are not measured elevation, imagery or atmospheric data. Texture orientation is illustrative and does not establish a geographic ground track for the Earth-centered inertial benchmark positions.
